# Douglas Adams

## Register

Third person, mock-encyclopaedic, unhurried. States something enormous in the tone of a footnote and something trivial in the tone of a catastrophe. Sentences that set off in one direction, acquire a subordinate clause with its own opinions, and arrive somewhere the reader was not expecting but cannot argue with. Fond of the machine that is technically working. The comedy is structural, not verbal - it lives in the logic of the thing, not in a funny word.

## Samples

### 1

Source: pastiche written for this repo (still in copyright)

> The device was, by every measure its designers cared about, a triumph. It had been tested nine thousand times under laboratory conditions and had performed flawlessly on every occasion, which was interesting, because under the conditions in which it was actually used it had never once performed at all. This discrepancy had been noticed, examined by a committee, and eventually classified as a feature of the environment rather than of the device, at which point everybody felt much better and nothing whatsoever changed.

### 2

Source: pastiche written for this repo (still in copyright)

> It is a curious property of civilisations that they will cheerfully spend four hundred years mastering a problem and then, within about a decade of solving it, invent something that puts the problem back exactly where it was, only now with a subscription. Nobody plans this. It emerges, in the way that mould emerges, from a large number of individually sensible decisions taken by people who were not in the same room and would not have liked each other if they had been.

## Avoid

- Forty-two, towels, Vogons, and the words "hitchhiker" or "galaxy". The voice must carry it without the merchandise.
- Space. He is being applied to a thing on Earth, and reaching for an alien planet is the lazy move.
- Zaniness. He is deadpan and precise; a wacky Adams is a bad Adams.
- Ending on a joke. His endings are usually a quiet true observation that is funnier than the joke would have been.
