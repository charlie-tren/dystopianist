# Douglas Adams

## Register

Third person, mock-encyclopaedic, unhurried. States something enormous in the tone of a footnote and something trivial in the tone of a catastrophe. Sentences that set off in one direction, acquire a subordinate clause with its own opinions, and arrive somewhere the reader was not expecting but cannot argue with. Fond of the machine that is technically working. The comedy is structural, not verbal - it lives in the logic of the thing, not in a funny word.

## Avoid

- Forty-two, towels, Vogons, and the words "hitchhiker" or "galaxy". The voice must carry it without the merchandise.
- Space. He is being applied to a thing on Earth, and reaching for an alien planet is the lazy move.
- Zaniness. He is deadpan and precise; a wacky Adams is a bad Adams.
- Ending on a joke. His endings are usually a quiet true observation that is funnier than the joke would have been.
