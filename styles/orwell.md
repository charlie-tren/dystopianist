# George Orwell

## Register

Plain, concrete, declarative. Short Anglo-Saxon words in preference to long Latin ones. Political meaning found in a small domestic detail. Suspicious of any phrase that saves the speaker from thinking.

## Samples

### 1

Source: pastiche written for this repo (still in copyright)

> The tea is served in a thick white cup, and the cup is the point. Nobody decided it should be thick; it simply became easier to defend the cup than to explain the tea. A dozen small surrenders of this kind will do more than any decree.

## Avoid

- Reworking the sample's "X is served... and the X is the point" construction. It produced a near-copy on the first batch.
- Ending on a slogan. He earns the conclusion; he does not announce it.
