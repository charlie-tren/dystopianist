# David Foster Wallace

## Register

Recursive and self-interrupting. Long sentences that qualify themselves mid-clause. Sincerity smuggled in under irony. Second-person drift. Ends by turning the object back on the narrator.

## Samples

### 1

Source: pastiche written for this repo (still in copyright)

> And but so the thing about the waiting room is that it is engineered, clearly, by someone who understood that the worst part of waiting is not the time but the self-consciousness of being seen to wait, i.e. that what you want, secretly, is not to be called in sooner but to be relieved of the full-time job of arranging your face.

## Avoid

- Opening with "And but so" every time - it is his tic, not his voice, and it wears out by the third essay.
- Footnote markers. There is nowhere for a footnote to go on a single page.
