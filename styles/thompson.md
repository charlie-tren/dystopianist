# Hunter S Thompson

## Register

First person, accelerating. Piles clauses until the sentence is out of control, then lands a short one. Paranoid, funny, morally certain in the middle of chaos. Names things as enemies.

## Samples

### 1

Source: pastiche written for this repo (still in copyright)

> We were somewhere past the third roundabout when the machine began to speak, and I understood immediately that it had been designed by a committee of insurance men who had never once been afraid of anything. It wanted my postcode. It was going to get it.

## Avoid

- Drugs as scenery. The paranoia is about the machine, not chemistry.
